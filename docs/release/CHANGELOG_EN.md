## 4.6.2 — first public release
- The Watcher: peeks at you from around corners, hides when you look at it, and reacts differently each time (stares back, slowly retreats, ignores you until you look away, or peeks out again).
- It comes by itself, more often in the dark and the longer you play; ambushes around corners in caves.
- Traces: torch trails and abandoned camps deep in caves, a door left open, villages empty for a day, a "dead day" with grey fog and no creatures. Nothing is lost for good.
- Forests: dead, torn-apart farm animals (decoration only) and rare footsteps or a rustle behind you.
- Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.x. Required on client and server. Settings: config/the1758645414-common.toml
